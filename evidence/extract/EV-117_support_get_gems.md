# EV-117 (full-page) — How can I get Gems?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003914938 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T20:38Z | closed for comments
FULL TEXT: "Gems can be obtained by completing league objectives, completing tournaments, through the Season Pass and Prize Ladder and by participating in Multiplayer (Dream League Live) mode. The quantity of Gems you receive will depend on the circumstance. You can also purchase Gems in the shop if you wish to acquire them more quickly (please seek the bill payer's permission)."
