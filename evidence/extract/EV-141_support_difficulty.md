# EV-141 (full-page) — How does game difficulty work?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018360418 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T22:43Z | closed for comments
FULL TEXT: "In single player mode difficulty in DLS starts easy and changes as users progress up and down the divisions in the game. This approach is common and typical to many video games. Additionally, in DLS25, we introduced the ability to change the base difficulty to either Medium or Hard. In Dream League Live (multiplayer) matches, users are always playing against another real user. No bots are used. There is no control of difficulty and there is no manipulation of any in-game situation, including team mate AI. There is no influencing of any outcome to any match. Our commitment to fair gaming and player respect can be found [ftgames.com/core-principles]. More on our Live matching can be found [matchmaking article]."
