# EV-151 (full-page) — How do I perform a skill move?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/213851489 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T23:28Z | closed for comments
FULL TEXT: "You can perform specific skill moves by swiping the right hand side of the screen (near the controls) whilst in possession of the ball. Swipe down for a Marseille turn, up for a rainbow flick and left/right for a stepover."
COACHING USE: three named skill moves with swipe directions (input layer for gameplay drills). DLS26 may add moves - volatile.
