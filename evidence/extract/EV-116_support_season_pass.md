# EV-116 (full-page extraction) — What is the Season Pass?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404070913169 | origin_method: official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T20:28Z | closed for comments
VERSION MARKER (verbatim): "NOTE: All information included below is applicable from our latest DLS release (version 12200 onwards)"
FULL OPERATIVE TEXT:
- Season Pass divided into two tiers: FREE (all users) and SEASON PASS (premium rewards, purchase via "Activate Pass!").
- Purchasing unlocks the Progress Bank, "which rewards currency based on your DLL (multiplayer) XP. The Progress Bank reward is given at the end of the season."
- Premium turns some profile elements gold (cosmetic).
- NOT a subscription; purchased per season; no auto-renew. Season end is shown by countdown timer in the Season Pass message box, "which details when the current season will end for all users."
- Pass lasts the entire season; late purchases still unlock all content given enough Season Points (tier-locks by calendar day removed for premium).
- "Season Points are earned from completing matches - the amount you earn will vary depending on the type of game mode that you are playing."
- Buying premium retroactively unlocks premium for tiers already earned on the free track.
- Free track: tier locks correspond to current season day; premium removes all tier locks.
