# EV-115 (full-page extraction, chunk 0/4) — How do the various player stats in DLS affect gameplay?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019166777 | origin_method: official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T20:28Z | comments paginated (chunks 1-3 OWED)
DISCLAIMER (verbatim gist): DLS is a game, not a full/accurate simulation; discovering how stats affect the game is part of the game, with "elements of unpredictability inherent in any real football match"; stats are intended to have meaningful bearing in line with real-life strengths.
STAT MODEL (operative content):
- SPE (Speed): top running speed; decreases as ENERGY reduces; decreases when dribbling inversely proportional to CON (high CON dribbles faster at same SPE); a lower-SPE player can outrun a higher-SPE player. See ACC.
- ACC (Acceleration): how quickly top speed is reached.
- STA (Stamina): energy depletion rate when running; affects half-time energy boost; matters more for high-distance positions (e.g. midfielder vs centre-back).
- CON (Ball Control): less speed loss when dribbling; quicker kick after first touch; more touches closer together (quicker direction change); ball kept closer; higher chance of skill kicks (Rabona, overhead), skill controls (heel-chops), skill shots (double tap or swipe, see SHO), skill moves (step-over, Marseille turn, heel-flick).
- STR (Strength): collision outcomes (less impacted, keep control/complete kick); shorter stumble after being tackled.
- TAC (Tackling): tackle success likelihood; distance of standing and sliding tackles.
- PAS (Passing): accuracy of all pass types.
- SHO (Shooting): shot success/accuracy; 'assist' moves shots away from keeper; keeping shots low (low SHO = higher/over bar); skill shot success (with CON); special kicks/headers chance; free-kick spin/curl.
- GKH (Keeper Handling): catch vs deflect likelihood; claiming crosses; stability under nearby opposition contact.
- GKR (Keeper Reactions): reaction speed; save chance vs shot speed and distance; 'anticipate' saves (save without time to react).
- ENERGY (battery icon): fatigue; below a threshold other stats reduce gradually as energy drops (speed most noticeable; tired players worse in all areas).
