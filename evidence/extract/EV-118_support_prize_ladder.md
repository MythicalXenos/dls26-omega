# EV-118 (full-page) — What is the Prize Ladder?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/23108987826962 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T20:38Z | closed for comments
FULL TEXT: "The Prize Ladder, similarly to the Season Pass, awards items based on your progression. The items can include - coins, gems, coaches, dream point boosts, special players and more! Dream Points are given in matches and the more your earn, the more rewards you will unlock. You can also purchase Dream Point Boosts which will temporarily increase the number of Dream Points that you are awarded during your matches."
NOTE: 'coaches' are named as Prize Ladder reward items — relevant to coach/fitness research (terminology to disambiguate vs Legendary Fitness).
