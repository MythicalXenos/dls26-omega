# EV-130 (full-page) — How do I backup/restore/transfer my save data?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/214387685 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T21:32Z | closed for comments
OPERATIVE CONTENT:
- For DLS (current), Ultimate Clash Soccer, Score! Hero, Score! Match: link profile to "Sign in with Google" or "Sign in with Apple" (iOS 13.0+) via game settings (manual, not automatic). Unsecured profiles may be unrecoverable after uninstall/reset/device change.
- Save data is NOT secured on Google Play Games or iCloud for these games ("different cloud saving services that are not used by our games").
- Older titles (DLS19): iCloud (iOS) / Google Play Saved Games (Android) only; no cross-platform transfer of save data or purchases.
- Setup guides admitted: Sign-in-with-Apple (360009450097), Sign-in-with-Google (9580096555281).
- Cross-game save articles (Ultimate Clash Soccer, Score Match, Score Hero) classified NON_CONNECTED.
