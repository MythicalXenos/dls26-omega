## 1. The mission, precisely

**"#1" = Tier 1 Champion in Dream League Live, in your own region, sustained across seasons.** DLS26 exposes no global #1 surface: the matchmaker looks only inside your regional pool (tier, location, availability), and the leaderboards reward performance, not squad value. A record run reached Tier 1 in **16 days** with a **mostly common/rare squad at 145,296 XP** — so the target is reachable without money, and the plan is built around that fact.

Thirty-five of the corpus's open gaps were closed during the turn-35 sweep, and **the single most productive discovery was that the official FTG support portal is fully readable** — it produced the stat model, the coin list, the account rules, the Season Pass rules, the player-acquisition routes and the leaderboard cadence, several of which are the only Confirmed-tier sources in the corpus.

## 5. Your first week, in order

1. **Link the account.** Google (Android) or Apple (iOS). Never sign out. Do not reset.
2. **Play six Career matches.** This unlocks Dream League Live and Friend Match, and pays coins and Season Points you need anyway.
3. **Claim the free Season Pass track** — 1,095 coins and an epic agent, no purchase.
4. **Stadium: build corners, one stand at a time**, toward 15,000 seats (the Division 3 gate).
5. **Training to Level 4** with your first 1,125 gems. Nothing else competes with it.
6. **Reach 16 players** — the club minimum — before you spend a single coach.
7. **Do not buy a legendary player.** It raises your team rating, which makes the cheap players disappear from the market and kills the coach-farming loop.
8. **Coach-farm at or below 192 coins** — and the sharper DLS26 filter is **130 or less**, using a 75-coin Common scout, released immediately. Budget ~32,000 coins per fully maxed player. **And never coach the interim squad: bank every coach until the XI is final.**
9. **Check the DLL season format**, then camp tiers 5–2 for gems. In an extra-time season, bank your wins before anything else.
10. **Place the squad by foot**: left-footed at LB/LCB, right-footed at RB/RCB; strikers inverted. Then spend your first coach.
11. **Learn the skill catalogue in Free Practice** — ten moves, each a specific swipe or tap, and each gated by the Control stat.

## 6. How to read a claim in this corpus

Every claim carries a **confidence tier** and at least one **evidence ID** (EV-xxx). **Confirmed** means an official First Touch source says it. **High** means multiple independent community sources agree. **Community** means one or two sources. **Speculative** means a single weak source — treat as a hypothesis. **Disputed** means sources actively conflict. Where this briefing and a claim record disagree, **the claim record wins** and this file should be corrected.
