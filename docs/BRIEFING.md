## 1. The mission, precisely

**"#1" = Tier 1 Champion in Dream League Live, in your own region, sustained across seasons.** DLS26 exposes no global #1 surface: the matchmaker looks only inside your regional pool (tier, location, availability), and the leaderboards reward performance, not squad value. A record run reached Tier 1 in **16 days** with a **mostly common/rare squad at 145,296 XP** — so the target is reachable without money, and the plan is built around that fact.

## 3. What the sweep closed, and how

Twenty-two of the corpus's open gaps were closed during the turn-35 sweep, and **the single most productive discovery was that the official FTG support portal is fully readable** — it produced the stat model, the coin list, the account rules, the Season Pass rules, the player-acquisition routes and the leaderboard cadence, several of which are the only Confirmed-tier sources in the corpus.

**Resolved outright:** Cult Heroes ratings (C-018 — the 92-vs-95 conflict was *three states of one card*: 85 base, 92 event-boosted, 95 maxed) · stadium capacity per division (C-010) · the achievement count (C-026 — a *source conflation*: Android 40/20,000 XP, iOS 63/13,483 EXP) · corner footedness (C-022 — *two deliveries, not two opinions*: same-foot out-swinger versus opposite-foot in-swinger) · the DLL unlock (C-025 — six Career matches).

**Explained rather than merely closed:** the formation meta (C-014 — shapes differ by failure mode; stamina beats shape) · the Commercial curve (C-019 — three curves on record, but the real magnitude is **~6–20 extra coins per game at max**, so spend there last) · the maxed-squad handicap (C-024 — **its official corroboration was withdrawn**: team strength is an *outcome* determinant, not a matching input, so it is community-only).

**Closed as documented negatives:** Form Boost per-level duration (assume **one match**) · Fan Approval mechanics (**no such system exists** — it is a readout of squad development, not a lever) · the Fanzone's per-level costs (exhausted on the open web).

**Corrected:** the squad cap is **64 players**, not 40 · the "5,625 gems for all five facilities" figure is a **DLS23-era** value · the roster rating ceiling is **86**.

## 5. Your first week, in order

1. **Link the account.** Google (Android) or Apple (iOS). Never sign out. Do not reset.
2. **Play six Career matches.** This unlocks Dream League Live and Friend Match, and pays coins and Season Points you need anyway.
3. **Claim the free Season Pass track** — 1,095 coins and an epic agent, no purchase.
4. **Stadium: build corners, one stand at a time**, toward 15,000 seats (the Division 3 gate).
5. **Training to Level 4** with your first 1,125 gems. Nothing else competes with it.
6. **Do not buy a legendary player.** It raises your team rating, which makes the cheap players disappear from the market and kills the coach-farming loop.
7. **Enter Dream League Live and camp tiers 5–2** for gems. Protect promotion streaks — every failed promotion wastes that tier's accumulated points.
8. **Place the squad by foot**: left-footed at LB/LCB, right-footed at RB/RCB; strikers inverted. Then spend your first coach.

## 6. How to read a claim in this corpus

Every claim carries a **confidence tier** and at least one **evidence ID** (EV-xxx). **Confirmed** means an official First Touch source says it. **High** means multiple independent community sources agree. **Community** means one or two sources. **Speculative** means a single weak source — treat as a hypothesis. **Disputed** means sources actively conflict. Where this briefing and a claim record disagree, **the claim record wins** and this file should be corrected.
