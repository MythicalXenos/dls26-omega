## Summary

Controller support added DLS25 (Dec 2024), carried into DLS26. Bluetooth pairing (PS5: Share+PS buttons to pair, then enable in-game controller settings). In-game: game-pad control settings with two profiles: 'Classic' (default) and 'Alternative' (custom); custom controller profiles supported; button mapping: Left Stick=Movement, A=Pass, B=Shoot, X=Through Pass, Y=Cross, RB/R1=Sprint, LB/L1=Switch Player (per third-party touch-mapping apps that overlay controls). Wired+wireless supported for broad gamepad compatibility.
