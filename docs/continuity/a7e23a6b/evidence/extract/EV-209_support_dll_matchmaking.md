# EV-209 (full 1/1) — OFFICIAL support: How does Dream League Live matchmaking work? (ref-support-dll-matchmaking) — MISSION CORE
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018359998-How-does-Dream-League-Live-matchmaking-work | official-support | fetched 2026-10-11T17:22Z
OPERATIVE CONTENT (verbatim core): LIVE mode matches players in real-time on **primarily their LIVE Tier ranking, their location, and the availability pool of other players at the time of matching.** Objective: opponents of similar standing & skill — generally same Tier, but cross-Tier when availability is low. **"There are no additional overlaid controls to manage the difficulty experience. Outcomes are governed by individual skill levels as reflected in their tactics, gameplay choices and team strength."** Higher Tiers = naturally more challenging opponents.
