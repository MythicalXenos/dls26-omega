# EV-121 (full-page) — What are Dream Point Boosts?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/24081168829330 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T20:53Z | closed for comments
FULL TEXT: "You can purchase Dream Point Boosts within the Prize Ladder, which will temporarily increase the number of Dream Points that you are awarded during your matches. Dream Point Boosts will only apply to career matches, dream draft matches, scenario matches and DLL (multiplayer) matches. They will not apply to exhibition and friend matches. Dream Point Boosts cannot be used if the Prize Ladder is complete or no longer active. Any unused Dream Point Boosts will remain in your inventory for the next Prize Ladder. Please note that all of the above is of course subject to change as we run live services."
