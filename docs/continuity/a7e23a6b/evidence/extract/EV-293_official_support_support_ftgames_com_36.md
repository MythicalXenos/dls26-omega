## Summary

DLL (Dream League Live): official matchmaking uses LIVE Tier ranking, geographic location, and availability pool as primary inputs (FTG support 360018359998); intent is similar-skill matches, same tier when possible. Weekly leaderboard resets (per BlueStacks, weekly cadence), awards gems by finishing position; gems also awarded for participation (play N matches/week). 'Tier camping' is a known community exploit: maxed-OVR players deliberately stay in Tiers 12-20 to be matched against weaker teams; community calls for team-rating-based matchmaking unaddressed. GCC (Global Challenge Cup) is ONLINE (under Career, per BlueStacks/es.wikipedia): round-robin groups vs other players, 10 gems per round cleared, resets regularly. Game modes total (es.wikipedia): Career (offline/online hybrid, 8 divisions), Online/DLL (vs global players, promotion/relegation by tier, tier championship = gems), Offline Exhibition (friendlies, no internet), Live Events (time-limited objectives → agents/coaches/coins).
