# EV-135 (full-page) — How does Dream League Live matchmaking work?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018359998 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T22:18Z | closed for comments
FULL TEXT: "Multiplayer LIVE mode 'matches' players in real-time based on a number of player-related attributes & elements. These are primarily their LIVE Tier ranking, their location and the availability pool of other players at the time of matching. The main objective in matchmaking is to try and ensure that every player is matched against an opponent of similar standing & skill as much as is possible... That should generally result in players playing against opponents of the same Tier, but occasionally that will not be possible due to the reduced availability of players. There are no additional overlaid controls to manage the difficulty experience. Outcomes are governed by individual skill levels as reflected in their tactics, gameplay choices and team strength. As players progress through the Tiers in LIVE mode, opponents will naturally become more challenging."
