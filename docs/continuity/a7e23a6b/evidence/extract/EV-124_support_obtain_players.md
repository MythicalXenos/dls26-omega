# EV-124 (full-page) — How do I obtain more players?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004066417 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T21:00Z | closed for comments
FULL TEXT: "You can obtain more players for your squad in different ways: Transfers - purchase your player here. New players appear each in-game week i.e. after playing a single player match. Scouts - Scouts can find a selection of players to purchase at a discounted rate. Agents - Agents will immediately add a player to your squad. Prize Ladder - the prize ladder awards special rare players when the criteria is met."
NOTE: transfer-market refresh is tied to playing a single-player match (each in-game week) — complements community 'activity-based refresh' claims.
