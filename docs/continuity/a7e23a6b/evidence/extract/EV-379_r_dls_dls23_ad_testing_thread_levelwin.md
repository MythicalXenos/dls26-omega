## Summary

Corrected/expanded economy numbers (from prior more-approximate claims):
- Ad rewards per day: up to 10 video-ad rewards per 24h reset; declining to watch an ad does NOT consume a slot (optimize by only doubling home wins).
- Post-match ad bonus doubles coins with min 30 / max 100 bonus on top of base (e.g. loss with 3c base → 3+30=33; big win with 110c base → 110+100=210). Stadium at 85k gives 48-52 coin per home win bonus; combined with ad this yields ~250c/home-win average.
- Team Boost (mid-game/halftime): 30 coins, restores ~10% stamina (same effect as post-game Team Physio).
- Team Physio default prices: Common 50c, Rare 100c, Legendary 200c (per team application); these are poor-value early because match coin rewards can't offset the cost; best saved for injury crunches.
- Starter pack (DLS24 data, corroborated for DLS26 direction): 1500 coins + 75 gems + free Common Scout + free Common Agent at captain-select/onboarding (previous 1200c/40g claim from gamingonphone was likely DLS20/DLS21 baseline; DLS24/DLS26 may be 1500/75 — NEED CONFIRMATION). Spend the 75 starter gems first on Training Centre (unlocks formations + -5% coach cost per earlier EV) not Agents.
- Rare players start appearing on Live Transfers at team rating ~65 OVR; Legendaries (80-82 OVR range) start appearing at ~68.7+ OVR team rating.
- Goals scored cap at 15 per match for coin/token rewards (scoring more than 15 yields nothing extra).
- Buy-and-release meta: buy 80-rated defenders (~1460c) or <180c players and immediately release for coach cards; with 85k stadium this yields ~250c per home match after ad doubling — fastest coin grind once stadium is maxed.
- Form Boost duration: starts at minimum 1 match; extended by Training Centre upgrades (FTG support).
- Daily login reward cycle is 20 days. Post-match ad x2 available once per match; menu 30c ad once per 24h. Daily ad limit ~10 video rewards (DLS23, may apply to DLS26).
