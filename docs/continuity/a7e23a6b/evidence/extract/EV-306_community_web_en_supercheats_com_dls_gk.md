## Summary

DLS GK controls (consistent across DLS versions per community): HOLD C button (loft/cross button) to rush goalkeeper out at attacker in 1-v-1 situations; red icon indicates GK is being manually controlled. Double-tap on your own goalkeeper to drop the ball at his feet (to play out short or dribble). When GK has the ball: B = long drop-kick / clearance; A = short throw/pass to nearest defender; C appears to be through-ball distribution (DLS button mapping B=shoot/long, A=pass/throw). Gamingonphone mentions 'Goalkeeper charge' as available in 1-on-1 situations. Note: DLS GK controls are simpler than FC26 (no manual dive-direction, no punch/catch cross-call, no GK cover-far-post modifiers) — rush + drop-ball + distribution only.
