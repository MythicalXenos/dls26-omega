# EV-169 (full-page) — Core Principles (ftgames.com)
source: https://www.ftgames.com/core-principles | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-11T01:08Z
OPERATIVE CONTENT (fair-gaming charter):
- No cheating tolerated: external software bots/tools/hacks OR bug exploitation = cheating; may block detected cheaters.
- Level playing field: "a game's AI will interact with our Players on a consistent and equal basis"; "We never pre-determine any result in advance, nor do we artificially bias the performance of one player when playing against another."
- Matchmaking "takes into account the relevant level achieved AND recent results" (NEW input vs EV-135 tier/location/pool — form-based component).
- Support 24-48h; unfair defects fixed; "defect" excludes expectation of success and perceived AI problems.
- Games NOT simulations: luck/unpredictability part for everyone alongside skill; AI influenced by tactics, passing/shooting quality, formation, development profile, positioning, predicted movement, weather, ball spin, stadium influence.
- Progression raises opponent competitiveness + AI demand; claims fairness throughout.
