# EV-129 (full-page) — Something happened in the game that's not realistic. Why is this?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000842697 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T21:32Z | closed for comments
FULL TEXT: "Dream League Soccer is a game and is not intended to be a fully accurate simulation of the sport or physics of soccer. Like many other sports games, Dream League Soccer aims to balance fun with realism, and you may find many unpredictable situations occurring, just like in real life. These may include unrealistic ball or player behaviour, or referee decisions that you don't agree with. In addition, like most games, luck forms a part of the experience. Ultimately it's the same for all players and above all else we consider our games 'fair'. We constantly review feedback and we do endeavour to fix things that we feel harm the enjoyment of the game. Not everything will be addressed however, and there will be things that players don't like that we accept as part of the game. ... In addition, we aim to fix any genuine bugs, so don't hesitate to contact us using the support link with details of any problems you identify."
IMPLICATIONS: luck/variance is BY DESIGN (official); 'fair/same for all players' is an official CLAIM (incentive context applies); genuine bugs are fixed via reports; some disliked behavior is accepted design. Coaching consequence: win/loss variance must not be over-attributed to strategy; experiments need variance handling (matches OMEGA-COACHING-001).
