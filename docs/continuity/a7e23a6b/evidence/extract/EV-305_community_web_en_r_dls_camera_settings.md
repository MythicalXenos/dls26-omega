## Summary

DLS26 camera types (selectable in Settings): TV (default/broadcast-style), Sideline (wide side-on), Ball (close-up following the ball), Goal-to-Goal (end-to-end vertical view), Tele Broadcast. Custom sliders: Camera Height and Camera Zoom (percentage 0-100%). Competitive players use Sideline @ ~30% zoom OR Goal-to-Goal @ 100% zoom; community recommends TV @ 80% distance (sportsdunia) or Tele Broadcast Height 75 / Zoom 35 (dreamleaguesoccerkits.com). Assist toggles confirmed: Kick Assist, Cross Assist, Pass Assist (all can be turned OFF for full manual). Auto Switch (player-cursor switch on defense): Semi Auto recommended. UI overlays: directional arrows ON/OFF, radar map ON/OFF, player names ON/OFF, scoreboard ON/OFF. Graphics/FPS options: Medium graphics + 60 FPS recommended for smooth play on most devices (lower-end devices reduce). Vibration/haptic toggle (OFF recommended).
