## Summary

DLS26 AI behavior community observations: (1) Launch (Dec 2025): passing was broken (100-rated passers felt like 42-rated bronze; balls floated); subsequent patch reverted passing physics to DLS25-like ('worse' per some users). DLS26 initial passing accounted for momentum/inertia which some considered realistic. (2) 'Hard' difficulty perceived AI handicap: AI wins balls 'artificially', user's players miss simple passes, throw-ins/shot power don't match input intensity. (3) AI defenders/GKs perceived as unpredictable; Donnaumma-tier GKs underperform; defenders sometimes 'do nothing' on certain shots. (4) DLS26 launch gameplay slower than DLS25 → control/passing/tackling stats gained value vs speed spam (fitness-coach spam discouraged). (5) C-button (lofted pass/cross) was nerfed at DLS26 launch. (6) Positive changes noted: less opponent 'lag', improved tiki-taka buildup, improved dribbling, reduced match scripting. (7) Negative points: worse player switching, 'stats feel placebo', defensive AI weaker than attacking AI, GKs unpredictable. (8) Recurring complaint: AI pressing aggressively; receiving players 'stuck' leading to interceptions; AI runs curve/run away from passes; high-rated players bullied off ball by lower-rated AI; low-tackle players dispossess high-control carriers. (9) Post-patch (Dec 18): AI perceived as 'useless again' / easier to score (reverted to first DLS26 update or worse). Long shots less effective than DLS25. (10) Legendary Division AI significantly harder than lower divisions; new players in Academy/Div4 can score 8-9 goals per game with 95-rated teams while Legendary Division is tight.
