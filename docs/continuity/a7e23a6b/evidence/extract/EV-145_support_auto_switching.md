# EV-145 (full-page) — Why are my players auto-switching all the time?
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008831518 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T22:58Z | closed for comments
FULL TEXT: "With default settings applied, control over players will automatically switch from one to the other when defending. This is to aid in getting your closest player to the ball to make a tackle and regain possession. If you wish to have a different level of control over players switching, open the settings menu (the gear icon in the top-left corner), tap on 'Controls' and change the 'Auto Switch' setting to the one that suits your play best."
COACHING RELEVANCE: Saad's pinned settings plan (form/camera/assist) should account for Auto Switch options; defensive switching is default-automatic.
