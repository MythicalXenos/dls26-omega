# EV-119 (full-page) — Multiplayer Troubleshooting
source: https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004222118 | official-stated | fetched 2026-10-10T20:46Z | closed for comments
OPERATIVE CONTENT:
- Multiplayer restricted to Wi-Fi or high-quality 5G/4G/3G (network technology must be on supported list).
- Matchmaking connection issues: check internet, retry later (opponent availability), use latest DLS version; some public Wi-Fi unsuitable.
- Anti-cheat claims: "In DLL matches, you and your opponent are connected to servers we run in the cloud. The netcode has been designed so that the quality of your opponent's connection has no impact on the quality of your experience. This means that lag switch cheats are not effective. Additionally your opponent is not informed of your network address which makes it very difficult for them to launch a Denial of Service attack on your device." Lag problems lie between your device and FTG servers (often last-mile).
- No Facebook friend invites; local multiplayer only same-platform + same Wi-Fi.
- IPv6: yes. 5G: yes. Data use: ~2MB per match (approximate).
- Latency/packet-loss sensitive (low bandwidth demand); different connection/time of day may help. Bluetooth off improves Wi-Fi matches. Support form: /hc/en-us/requests/new.
