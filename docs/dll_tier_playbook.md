## 0. The two facts that change how you play

### 0.1 There is no hidden difficulty — and that cuts both ways

Official FTG matchmaking statement [EV-447, **Confirmed**]:

> LIVE mode matches players in real time based on a number of player-related attributes and elements, **primarily their LIVE Tier ranking, their location and the availability pool** of other players at the time of matching. […] **There are no additional overlaid controls to manage the difficulty experience.** Outcomes are governed by individual skill levels as reflected in their tactics, gameplay choices and **team strength**. […] As players progress through the Tiers, opponents will naturally become more challenging.

Three consequences:

1. **No rubber-banding.** If you lose, it is because of tactics, execution, or squad — not a hidden handicap. That is good news: everything is fixable.
2. **Team strength is a matching input.** This is the official basis for the maxed-squad handicap below.
3. **Same-tier matching sometimes fails.** On low player availability you will face opponents well outside your tier. A "mismatch" is not a bug; budget for it.

### 0.2 Maxing your squad can hurt you in Live (C-024)

A Tier 1 player [EV-448, C2]:

> A squad like yours is perfect, slightly coached players but not close to being maxxed (**maxxed squads perform TERRIBLY in high tiers due to handicap**). […] Just try not to coach your players anymore, trust me, it just ruins you long term.

Corroborated by the official matchmaking input above, and by multiple reports of players stuck at tiers 4 and 10 with part-maxxed and fully-maxxed squads.

**What this means in practice — and it is not "never coach":**

| Goal | Squad target |
|---|---|
| **DLL Tier 1** | **Competitive but NOT maxed.** Slightly coached beats maxed. |
| **Club value / Prize Ladder** | Maxed — 23 players at 100% is the definition of 100% club value. |

These are **two separate optimisation targets that pull in opposite directions.** The plan is to run them in parallel, not to let one gate the other: coach to competitive for Live, and pursue full maxing as an independent club-value project. The record Tier 1 run used a **mostly common/rare squad** [EV-432], which is the strongest single piece of evidence that maxing is not a prerequisite for the top tier.

**Confidence note:** this is the most consequential open conflict in the KB. It is Community Consensus with an official mechanism behind it, not a datamined fact. Treat the *direction* as reliable and the *threshold* (how much coaching is too much) as unknown.

## 2. Band-by-band tactics

### Tiers 20–15 — build the habits, ignore the result

**Objective:** mechanics, not wins.

| Do | Why |
|---|---|
| **Turn auto-switch OFF** and practise in Career first | It will feel wrong and you will lose some matches. Do it here, not in Live. [EV-441, C2] |
| Drill **corners and free kicks in Exhibition** | Corners are the most reliable goal source in DLS26. [EV-444, C2/C3] |
| Practise **passing in the Corner training mode** | The passing drill restarts when you lose the ball — it is the cleanest repetition available. |
| Learn **backwards running** as a defensive habit | It solves both anti-wing and anti-C-spam. [EV-441, C2] |

> At tier 16–15, opponents have "zero field awareness and passing skill." If you are struggling here, the problem is your mechanics, not your squad.

### Tiers 15–11 — the climb

**Objective:** advance one tier at a time, deliberately.

- **Expect a mismatch at 15.** You will face maxed teams. It is a matching artifact, not a skill check.
- **Do not sit still.** The tier reportedly gets harder if you do not advance — *"if you don't advance, then the tier you're playing gets harder… you have to go down first."* [EV-448, C3]
- **Keep the squad competitive, not maxed.** See §0.2.

### Tier 11 → 10 — the cliff

The single most reported wall in the game: *"stuck in tier 10 and it was so difficult as the players are getting much stronger after passing tier 11."* [r/DLS rsrzfa]

**What to do at the cliff:**

1. **Do not panic-promote.** Getting over the cliff into a tier where you cannot survive wastes the run.
2. **Check your squad is competitive, not maxed.** A maxxed squad is reported to perform terribly here specifically.
3. **Sub late for pace.** Last-minute goals are prevented by subbing on pacy centre-backs and keeping control of them. [r/DLS 1b9m85y]
4. **Do not tap or hold B unless you are in front of the ball.** Committing your defender is what gets you lobbed.

### Tiers 10–6 — competitive

- **Tier 10 is the Live gem floor.** Below it you earn nothing from Live. Never plan to sit below tier 10.
- Tier 8 is where "plenty of legendary teams" appear and you *"need to become more reactive."* [r/DLS 1b9m85y]
- Tier 4 is a second wall for part-maxxed squads — *"I have like a 1/3rd maxxed team and it gets tough for me in tier 4. Never reached above it."*

### Tiers 5–2 — THE CAMPING ZONE (the gem engine)

**This is the most important section in this document.**

> **Camp between Tier 5 and Tier 2. Tier 1 cannot be camped because it is the ceiling.** [EV-448, C2]

**The rule: aim for SURVIVAL, NOT PROMOTION.**

- Survival = win **4–5 matches** (12–15 points).
- Then **quit the rest** — and *losing en route is even better*, because it keeps you in the camping tier instead of promoting you into a harder one.
- **Do not forfeit.** Forfeiting may prevent you receiving the maximum gems. [EV-448, C3]

**The economics** [EV-448, C3]:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Matches | ~48 |
| Time | 4 – 4.8 hours |
| Gems from DLL | ~150 |
| 4-hour minimum | 160 – 208 gems |
| **Rate** | **~35–43 gems per hour** |

For comparison, the daily challenge set pays **5–11 gems per day**. Tier camping is roughly **30× more efficient per hour of play**.

**The one caveat:** this depends on being good enough to survive at tiers 2–5. If you cannot survive there, camp higher (tier 6–8) where survival is easier but the gem return is lower. The camping tier is a dial, not a fixed setting.

**Re-entry rule:** if you get promoted out of the camping zone, you have two options — deliberately drop back (see §3), or accept the promotion and camp at the new, harder tier. Do not drift; decide.

### Tier 1 — the goal

**You cannot camp here.** You either hold it or you do not.

**Tier 1 play is about SPACE, not players** [EV-448, C2]:

1. **Open with 3-4-1-2 attacking.** On kickoff you immediately have **six players running at goal, four of them viable passing options**. If the pass is not on, tap the map and switch to defensive.
2. **Keep it simple.** Pass to the open player, pass again when someone presses him. KISS.
3. **Read the gaps, not the defenders.** *"I visualize the defenders as moving cones, and see the correct passing lane, the gap present between two defenders. Passes of this manner are difficult for my opponents to intercept, because they don't know which of the two defenders to switch to."* This is the highest-skill idea in the corpus and it is a direct exploit of the switching mechanic.
4. **Think two to three passes ahead.** The test: from a dead ball in the opponent's half, can you reach a good shot in **under five passes**? Practise this on free kicks.
5. **Formations that cover the middle vertically** without wasting players on the wings: **4-1-2-1-2, 3-4-1-2, 3-2-3-2.** These make life hard for central tiki-taka players.

**Tier 2 note:** opponents at tier 2 are at **10M+ XP**. Expect them to know all of the above.

## 3. Deliberate demotion — when and why

Widespread and acknowledged [EV-448, C3]:

> Keep losing the matches, then drop to tier 12 or below. After all, begin to gain xp until you feel like dropping tiers again, repeating the same tricks. […] there's lots of players deliberately quitting or losing the match for getting legendary players at the lower tiers.

**Legitimate uses:**

| Use | Why |
|---|---|
| **Event farming** | Events are easier to complete at a low DLL tier. *"staying in the lowest Division for DLS Live or 1 rank above that so you can win some events"* |
| **Legendary player drops** | Weaker opponents at low tiers mean more wins and more market activity |
| **XP missions** | A 150-win mission is far easier against weak opponents |

**Rule:** deliberate demotion is a *tool*, not a default. Use it deliberately for a stated objective, then climb back. Drifting down without a reason wastes the climb.

## 4. Defending in Live — the DLL-specific model

Career defending and Live defending are not the same game.

| Rule | Detail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Tackling is worthless; speed is everything** | *"in live matches stats like tackling are worthless if you can catch up to opponent"* — use 95+ speed and acceleration players as defenders (Salah, Son, Kyle Walker named) | EV-448, C2 |
| **Fastest attackers at centre-back** | Nuno Mendes and Hakimi are better used at CB than at wing-back | EV-448, C3 |
| **Real centre-backs stay for corners** | Proper CBs give you height on the most reliable goal source in the game | EV-444, C2/C3 |
| **Full-backs do not chase** | They make sliding tackles instead | EV-448, C3 |
| **Defensive 4-3-1-2 beats C-spam** | Slow build-up play limits concessions and disrupts the C-spam rhythm | EV-448, C3 |
| **Do not hold the main press** | It gives the C-spammer the advantage; use the secondary press and cover the goal | EV-448, C2 |
| **Sub pacy CBs late** | Last-minute goals are prevented by subbing pace on and keeping control | r/DLS 1b9m85y, C3 |

**Note on the tension with coaching:** the STR/CON ×2 coaching weighting is a *general* rule, but in Live specifically, **speed beats strength**. Do not let the ×2 weighting talk you out of a fast defender.

## 5. Career, in service of Live

Career is the coin engine. DLL is the gem engine. Do not confuse them.

| Rule | Detail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **The Easy-difficulty shortcut** | Set Easy, let the first half play out unattended (AI scores 2–4), score exactly **5**, finish. Roughly half the time for **120 + 100** in a Legendary division home game. | EV-448, C2 |
| **Score exactly 5, never 7** | Goal coins cap at **30 per match** (6 × 5). A 7th goal is wasted time. | EV-441, C2 |
| **Stadium pacing** | Upgrade the stadium **only to the minimum required for each division**, then stop playing Career until you like your team. Stadium upgrades eat the coins you need elsewhere. | EV-448, C3 |
| **Win by 4+ for Season Points** | SP drives the Season Pass. | EV-437, C2 |
| **Max the Medical Centre early** | Reduces the injury probability for your starting eleven "to a great extent." | gamingonphone DLS25, C3 |

## 6. DLS25/DLS26 gameplay shifts to re-check

DLS25 changed the physics in ways that partly **contradict the older advice still circulating** [EV-448, C3]:

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Foot dominance matters** | Preferred foot affects turning and passing fluency |
| **Narrow-space passes are harder** | Tight combinations are less reliable |
| **Long-range shots are less effective** | The old "long shots >75% accuracy" and long-B meta advice is version-specific and may no longer hold |

**What this pushes you toward:** width and positional play, phased build-up, and creating clear chances in the box rather than forcing shots from outside.

> **Confidence note:** these shifts are reported for DLS25 (Dec 2024) and have not been re-verified for DLS26. The KB retains both the old long-shot advice and this newer position; they are in tension and the newer one is more likely correct for the current build. **Verify in Exhibition before relying on either.**

## 7. The one-page DLL routine

**Daily (≈45–60 min):**
1. Season Pass login (+40 SP) and the **full** daily challenge set (5–11 gems)
2. Daily Scenario (50 coins)
3. **3–5 DLL matches at tier 10 or above**
4. **Tier-camp block when gems are the priority:** ~48 matches over 4–4.8 hours for 150–208 gems, aiming for survival not promotion
5. Buy 3–5 players under 210 coins → release → coaches
6. One 8-hour ad cycle (up to 600 coins)
7. Two corner or free-kick attempts in Exhibition

**Weekly:** full weekly challenge set · clan contribution toward 350k · Progression Bank check.

**The decision you make every session:** *am I farming gems (tier camp, tiers 5–2) or climbing (push one tier)?* Never both in the same block — camping punishes promotion and climbing punishes deliberate loss.
