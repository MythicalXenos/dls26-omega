# Irreversible and consequential action list

**Status:** Provisional candidate list only; Step 1 is incomplete, and no action type has been exhaustively investigated. These entries are research leads, not settled game facts or current advice. Confidence tiers are Speculative. The list is not evidence that the action is available in the user's current account.

## Candidate action types already surfaced by retrieved sources

### Signing out of Google or Apple while a DLS profile is linked
- **Why it may be consequential:** FTG's single help-center article says signing out at any point unlinks the account from the game profile and may expose it to loss if the app is uninstalled or the device is changed. The exact recovery path and current-client behavior are unverified.
- **Source:** `page-003-ftg-save-data-transfer`, https://support.ftgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413273241873-How-is-save-data-stored-and-transferred-in-DLS (fetched 2026-10-02; search metadata date 2024-12-06).
- **Confidence:** Speculative (one official support article; current applicability/corroboration open).
- **Before doing it:** Confirm profile-link status and current FTG instructions; do not sign out, uninstall, reset, or change devices based on this single article without checking current account recovery implications. No user intent to do this has been expressed.
- **Unresolved:** Whether sign-in restores the link, exactly which actions are required for profile loss, and whether there is any current-client exception.

### Allowing a live special-player storefront event to expire without checking the in-game offer
- **Why it may be consequential:** Google Play's Cult Heroes event page says “Ends on 10/14,” calls it limited-time, and urges players to obtain the items now; the year is not displayed. Apple labels the event “HAPPENING NOW / LIVE EVENT” and says the featured players have boosted attributes. Missing an actual time-limited offer could remove an acquisition opportunity, but the in-game availability and date are not verified.
- **Sources:** `page-009-googleplay-cult-heroes-event` and `page-010-apple-cult-heroes-event`, fetched 2026-10-02.
- **Confidence:** Speculative (storefront text only; exact date/year, region and in-game offer details open).
- **Before doing it:** Check the game's own event page, the exact expiry timestamp, player list and cost. Do not spend resources based only on storefront text. The user has said they are pursuing an unspecified special player on a prize ladder, but has not stated an intention to acquire a Cult Heroes player or spend on this specific offer.
