## PART B — What "#1" actually means here (read once, then forget it)

I need you to know the target, because it changes what you optimise for.

**The target is Tier 1 Champion in your own region — the SEA/Asia pool — held across consecutive seasons.** Not a global #1. DLS26 does not publish a global ranking surface, so chasing one means optimising against a metric the game never shows you.

**The thing that matters most about this:** the leaderboard rewards **performance, not squad value**. A documented world-record run reached Tier 1 Champion in **16 days** with a *mostly common and rare team* — 110 wins, 10 losses, 1 draw, 145,296 XP, starting deliberately from the bottom tier. [EV-432, C3]

So the plan does not begin with buying players. It begins with playing correctly, and with protecting your coin income. If you remember one sentence from this document, make it that one.

## PART C — Tonight: your first 60 minutes

Do these in order. Do not skip ahead.

### C1 — Claim and set up (5 min)

1. **Claim the starter grant: 1,500 coins and 75 gems.** This is a one-time thing.
2. **Pick your best attacker as captain.** This is not cosmetic — the captain boosts your team rating, and a higher rating improves *who appears in the transfer market*. [EV-441, C2]
3. Set your manager name, club name, kit and logo.
4. **Do not import a kit tonight.** Kit import costs 150 gems and needs a 512×512 image. You have 75 gems. It can wait. [EV-437, C2]

### C2 — First matches (20 min)

5. Play Career matches and **win them**. Do not chase a seventh goal — goal coins are **capped at 30 per match** (6 goals × 5). [EV-441, C2]
6. Win by **4 or more goals** where you can. This is what maximises Season Points, and Season Points drive the Season Pass.
7. Play the whole match rather than skipping. You are learning the controls, and stamina management only makes sense once you feel it.
8. **Rotate.** A player recovers **30% stamina** per match played and **fully** on the bench. Sub at **60–70 minutes**. You get **3 subs**. [EV-437, C2]

### C3 — The daily economy (15 min)

This is the part most players skip, and it is worth more than any match you will play tonight.

9. **Log in, and log in every single day.** The login cycle is **20 days** and it **restarts from Day 1 if you miss one**. Missing a day costs you the whole run. [EV-445, C2]
10. **Complete the FULL daily challenge set** — not just the easy ones. The *set completion* is what pays: **5–11 gems per day** and roughly 70–100 coins. [EV-445, C2]
11. If a daily objective is bad, **reroll it. You get 2 rerolls per day** (2 per week for weekly objectives). Use them — they are free. [EV-445, C2]
12. **Play the Daily Scenario.** 50 coins. Fair warning: you start *losing* and must beat the Hard AI. The community calls scenarios the hardest content in the game. Budget real time for it, and do not be discouraged if it takes a few attempts. [EV-445, C2]
13. **Start one 8-hour ad cycle.** Up to **600 coins**. Come back to it. [EV-437, C2]

### C4 — First purchases (10 min)

14. **Buy 1–2 fast common or rare players.** Nothing more. You need a squad that can win games; you do not need a good squad yet.
15. **Do NOT buy a legendary player tonight.** This is the single most expensive mistake available to you on day one, and it is not obvious:

> Buying a legendary raises your team rating. A higher rating makes the **cheap players disappear from the transfer market**. Those cheap players are the raw material for the coach-farming loop that actually builds your squad. Buy legendaries too early and you break your own engine. [EV-441, C2]

16. **Spend nothing else.** Keep the coins.

### C5 — Before you close the app (10 min)

17. Check the **Season Pass**. You should have progress toward the 40 SP/day target.
18. Note your **Division Objectives** — these gate promotion, and they are easy to miss.
19. If you have 75 gems left and nothing urgent, **put them into the Training Centre, level 1 (75 gems, +5%).** It is the cheapest first step and the training ladder is what makes coaches affordable later.

## PART D — The first week

| When | Do this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Every day** | Log in · full daily challenge set · daily scenario · one ad cycle | The login cycle resets on a miss; the challenge set is your gem income |
| **Every day** | 3–5 DLL matches, **at tier 10 or above** | Tier 10 is the Live gem floor — below it you earn nothing |
| **Every day** | Buy 3–5 players under 210 coins → **release** them | **Release yields coaches, not coins.** This is the loop. |
| **Week 1** | Reroll bad objectives (2/day) | Free value |
| **Week 1** | Check the **stadium class requirement** for promotion | Promotion has a stadium gate *in addition to* your league position [EV-437, C2] |
| **Week 1** | Start the **Progression Bank** and keep it alive | It is a 10-day bank — it only pays if you keep playing daily [EV-437, C2] |
| **Ongoing** | Buy common players at **≤180–210 coins**, and **80-rated defenders** for rares | Defenders are the cheapest 80s [EV-437, C2] |
| **Ongoing** | Train at a **7:3 or 90:10 fitness:technical** ratio | The ratio that matters [EV-437, C2] |

**One thing to understand about the market, because it will save you weeks:** the transfer market **refreshes after every match you play**. But **forfeiting a Career match, playing scenarios, playing exhibition matches, watching free ads, and Dream Draft all earn coins WITHOUT refreshing it.** [EV-442, C2/C3]

That means when you are saving for a specific player, farm coins via scenarios and ads — your target stays pinned in their slot instead of being shuffled away. This is the correct answer to *"I can't afford the player I want."*

And when you *do* want a refresh fast: **quit a Live match at half-time.**

## PART E — The gem decision you will face soon

You will reach a point with a few hundred gems and a long list of things to buy. Here is the order.

**Buy the Training Centre to level 4 before anything else in the facility list.**

| Level | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | 75 gems | +5% |
| L2 | 150 gems | +10% |
| L3 | 225 gems | +15% |
| **L4** | **300 gems** | **Legendary coaches drop below 200 coins — this is the unlock** |
| L5 | 375 gems | +25% |

L4 costs 750 gems cumulative, and it **inverts your entire coach economy**. Every other facility purchase is marginal by comparison. [EV-437, C2]

**Facility priority after that:** Stadium → Medical → Commercial → Fanzone. Note that the *first tier* of every facility costs only **375 coins** in DLS26 — take those cheap entry tiers early with coins, and save gems for the ladder. [EV-437, C2]

## PART F — Five things to do, five things not to

### Do

1. **Log in every day.** The 20-day cycle punishes a single miss.
2. **Complete full challenge sets.** The set, not the individual objective, is what pays.
3. **Release players for coaches.** This is your rating engine, and it costs coins not gems.
4. **Hold B while defending** and leave the joystick alone. It is the single highest-value mechanical habit in the game. [EV-441, C2]
5. **Practise corners and free kicks in Exhibition.** Corners are the most reliable goal source in DLS26 — a December 2025 post put it as *"if your opponent in DLL gets one, it's almost guaranteed you concede."* Exhibition costs nothing and gives you a free training ground. [EV-444, C2/C3]

### Do not

1. **Do not buy legendary players before the stadium is maxed.** See C4.15 — it kills your coach loop.
2. **Do not chase a 7th goal.** Goal coins cap at 30 per match. [EV-441, C2]
3. **Do not look for a youth academy.** It does not exist in DLS26. [GS-023]
4. **Do not trust mods, generators, or "unlimited coins."** They get your account blocked or steal from you. Official FTG position: infinite coins and gems do not exist, mods mean account blocking, generators are scams. [OFFICIAL, GS-020]
5. **Do not chase the formation meta.** It is genuinely unsettled. Pick one formation and learn it deeply — the record Tier 1 run used a mostly common/rare squad, so formation-chasing is not what separates you from the field. [C-014, EV-432]
