## Addendum (turn 12, 2026-09-28): "C-SPAMMING" — the DLS Live lofted-through-ball meta (S-0058, All-Methods: documented with risk framing; a gameplay technique, not a cheat)
dlsmod.com/c-spamming-in-dls/ (images dated Dec-2025): C-spamming = repeatedly lofting the ball over the opponent's defensive line with the **C button** (lofted through pass) to a fast/tall striker, exploiting AI-defender hesitation. Full loop: (1) attackers: tall + high speed/acceleration — listed: Haaland, Mbappé, **Batistuta**, Kane, Dembélé, **Bergkamp, Zola** (three ladder classics are meta attackers!); (2) set tactics to ATTACKING via the live-formations icon (blue "ATTACKING" dialogue); (3) after kickoff pass to CBs and push the whole team + CBs forward; (4) C-button loft with power gauge "a little more than half"; (5) immediately toggle to the attacker and dash — the run beats the offside trap because it starts while the ball is airborne; (6) finish the 1v1 vs the keeper who rushes out to narrow the angle. **Weaknesses (defensive counter-play for the user):** the attacker's own defence sits in offensive mode and is equally exposed to the same loft/through-ball; kit-colour confusion can be used against opponents who forget to differentiate. Community labels it "unfair" but it is engine mechanics ("learn how to defend it"). User-facing later (advisory phase): recognize the setup (high line + ATTACKING toggle + aerial launches) and bait the counter.
