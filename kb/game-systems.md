# KB — GAME SYSTEMS (official semantics)
Official FTG-stated mechanics from support articles fetched in full 2026-10-17, unless a claim carries its own tier. Claim IDs GS-001…GS-011.

- **GS-001 Player stats (official semantics)** — FTG article 360019166777. SPE Speed: top running speed; falls as ENERGY drops; falls when dribbling, inversely to CON (a high-CON player dribbles faster than a low-CON player with equal SPE; a lower-SPE player can outrun a higher-SPE one). ACC Acceleration: how quickly top speed is reached. STA Stamina: energy depletion rate while running; also the half-time energy boost; position-dependent relevance (a CB covers far less distance than a midfielder). CON Ball Control: less dribbling speed loss; quicker kick after first touch; closer successive touches (quicker direction changes); touches keep the ball closer; higher chance of skill kicks (Rabonas, Overhead Kicks), skill controls (heel-chops), skill shots (double-tap/swipe, alongside SHO), skill moves (Step-over, Marseilles turn, Heel-Flick). STR Strength: collision impact — higher means less impacted, better at gaining/maintaining the ball or completing a kick; shorter stumble after being tackled. TAC Tackling: success likelihood; standing- and sliding-tackle distances. PAS Passing: accuracy of all pass types. SHO Shooting: more successful shots; accuracy on all shot types; influences the "assist" that moves a shot away from the keeper; keeps shots low (low SHO → shots rise, potentially over the bar); skill-shot success (with CON); special kick/header chance (Overhead kick, Diving header); free-kick spin/curl. GKH Keeper Handling: handling/presence; catch-vs-deflect likelihood; claiming crosses; stability in contact when saving. GKR Keeper Reactions/shot stopping: reaction time; save chance relative to shot speed and distance; "anticipate" saves (saving without time to react). ENERGY/Battery: fatigue; below a threshold the other stats reduce gradually, most visibly speed. FTG framing: DLS is a game, not a full simulation; part of the game is discovering how stats behave amid inherent unpredictability. Confidence: Confirmed (official-stated). Volatility: Low (canonical article, ~4 years old, still current) — re-verify against current datamining in Step 2 before high-stakes use.
- **GS-002 Difficulty** — FTG article 360018360418. Single-player: difficulty starts easy and changes as the player moves up and down the divisions; a base-difficulty option (Medium or Hard) exists since DLS25. Dream League Live: always against another real user; NO bots; NO difficulty control; NO manipulation of any in-game situation including teammate AI; NO influencing of any match outcome. Fair-gaming commitment: ftgames.com/core-principles. Confidence: Confirmed. Consequence: DLL pressure comes from opponent skill variance, not difficulty manipulation — the Online Pressure Clause's "online play creates pressure that career mode doesn't" is explained by real opponents, not rigged difficulty. Volatility: Low.
- **GS-003 Economy** — Gems (article 360003914938): earned from league objectives, tournaments, the Season Pass, the Prize Ladder, and Dream League Live participation; quantity varies by circumstance; purchasable in shop. Coins: earning article 214385285 exists (body not yet fetched — owed). Dream Points (article 23108987826962): awarded in matches; more earned → more rewards unlocked on the Prize Ladder. Dream Point Boosts (article 24081168829330): purchased within the Prize Ladder; temporarily increase Dream Points awarded per match; apply to career, dream draft, scenario and DLL (multiplayer) matches ONLY — not exhibition or friend matches; unusable while the Prize Ladder is complete/inactive; unused boosts carry to the next Prize Ladder; all subject to change as a live service. Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: High (live-service values).
- **GS-004 Season Pass** — article 4404070913169 (applicable from version 12.200 onwards). Two tiers: FREE (all users) and SEASON PASS (premium purchase). Purchasing unlocks the Progress Bank, which rewards currency based on DLL (multiplayer) XP, paid at season end. Premium turns profile elements gold. NOT a subscription — must be purchased each season; no auto-renewal. Season Points are earned by completing matches; the amount varies by game mode. Tier locks correspond to days; buying mid-season instantly unlocks premium rewards for tiers already reached; premium removes all tier locks. Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: High (per-season).
- **GS-005 Prize Ladder** — article 23108987826962. Awards items on progression, similarly to the Season Pass: coins, gems, coaches, Dream Point Boosts, special players and more; special rare players when criteria are met; driven by Dream Points earned in matches. Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: High (per-ladder).
- **GS-006 Recruitment** — article 360004066417. Transfers: purchase players; new players appear each in-game week, i.e. after playing a single-player match. Scouts: find a selection of players to purchase at a discounted rate. Agents: immediately add a player to the squad. Prize Ladder: awards special rare players when criteria are met. Confidence: Confirmed. → "In-game week" is the match-based clock (partially resolves the prompt's "reset cycles" — see terminology T-020).
- **GS-007 Clans** — article 30839289076626. One clan at a time; each clan has one leader (leadership passes on; an empty clan disappears); some clans set entry requirements (hidden from search unless met; joinable via invite code); Clan Points are earned through matches, challenges and season passes; FTG may remove players or entire clans that break the Terms of Use or create a negative experience; disputes inside a clan are not mediated by FTG. Confidence: Confirmed (existence and rules); deeper mechanics (clan matches, rewards structure) still owed. Volatility: High.
- **GS-008 Coaching / player development** — official article 360003914778: Coaches (Team Management screen) randomly select a player and PERMANENTLY upgrade them. Form Boost: a more powerful but TEMPORARY upgrade lasting a set number of matches, the duration scaled by the Training facilities level (minimum one match). Community detail (multiple Reddit threads across seasons + gamingonphone + bluestacks — Community Consensus): coach categories Fitness (SPE/ACC/STA/STR), Technical (CON/PAS/SHO/TAC), Goalkeeping (GKR/GKH), Special (offers two randomized stat choices); rarities Common (+1 random stat), Rare (+2 stats by +2), Legendary (+3 stats by +3); coaches hired with gems or by releasing players; development ceiling = base OVR +10 (rarely +11); common ordering advice: fitness before technical; group-training 16–20 players. sakibpro simulator (tool-derived, single source — Speculative): DLS26 uses weighted progression energy (CON/SPE cost more capacity than STA); 100% cumulative weight = the +10 ceiling. Volatility: High (tuning changes per season).

Community comments report: no rewards when the opponent quits; occasional connect failures. Confidence: Confirmed (mechanic, official-stated); community-anecdote for the quit/reward behavior. - **GS-015 Save data & accounts** — one DLS account per player; "Manage Devices" and "Link Profile" under Options (gear icon, top left) → Advanced tab; transfer across devices via Sign in with Google (Android only) or Sign in with Apple (iOS only); cross-platform transfer (Android↔iOS) via generated code — Generate Code on the old device, enter in Link Profile on the new; a code cannot be used on the device that generated it; linked devices are listed and removable; signing out of Google/Apple at any point unlinks the profile and makes it susceptible to loss on uninstall or device change; codes and details are unrecoverable if lost and must not be shared (article 4413273241873). ## Turn-5 additions (verified 2026-10-17)
- **GS-024 Multiplayer networking** — DLL multiplayer requires Wi-Fi or high-quality 5G/4G/3G; FTG runs DLL matches on its own cloud servers; the netcode is designed so the opponent's connection quality has NO impact on your experience — lag-switch cheats are not effective; the opponent is not informed of your network address (DoS protection); persistent lag indicates an issue between the device and FTG's server, usually the last mile; no Facebook-friend invites (local multiplayer possible same-platform over the same Wi-Fi router); IPv6 and 5G supported; ~2MB data per match; disabling Bluetooth improves Wi-Fi multiplayer (article 360004222118). Confidence: Confirmed (official-stated). Volatility: Low. → the official architecture statement relevant to community "scripts"/lag-cheat perceptions (T-027).
- **GS-025 Player database & licensing** — the database is constantly updated: players are added and removed; some players cannot be included for licensing reasons (article 213852049). Likenesses and commentary names are likewise licensing-limited — user-stated examples in the comments of article 360004718318: some players lack faces, some names are not called by commentators, Bundesliga/German players absent. Confidence: Confirmed (licensing gaps exist); the specific examples are user-stated. Volatility: Critical (roster changes per update).
- **GS-026 Selling/releasing players** — from version 12.200 onwards: "Manage Players" (bottom right of the Transfers screen) → select player → release; a coach is received for the release; recently sold players appear in the "Recover" section of "Manage Players" for a limited time (article 360003945617). Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: Low. → the Recover section is a safety window for accidental releases.
- **GS-027 Video clips / ads** — multiple ad networks manage their own content; FTG controls only ad types (e.g., blocking explicit content); there is never a promise or guarantee of clip frequency or reward doublers; Limit Ad Tracking (LAT) reduces ad availability (networks are paid on conversions; exacerbated on iOS 14+); troubleshooting: latest app version, uninstall/reinstall (back up the profile first), disable VPN, use Wi-Fi instead of mobile data, restart the device, force-close regularly, wait a few days (article 360017166918). Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: High (ad availability).
- **GS-028 Data usage** — online required for the majority of features; FTG strongly recommends Wi-Fi and limiting mobile data (article 360005680437). Confidence: Confirmed (thin article). Volatility: Low.

## Turn-6 additions (verified 2026-10-17)
- **GS-031 Officially stated fairness & unpredictability stance** — FTG article 360000842697 ("Something happened in the game that's not realistic. Why is this?"): DLS is a game, not a fully accurate simulation of soccer or its physics; FTG balances fun with realism; unpredictable situations occur "just like in real life" — unrealistic ball or player behaviour, referee decisions the player disagrees with; "like most games, luck forms a part of the experience"; "Ultimately it's the same for all players and above all else we consider our games 'fair'"; FTG reviews feedback and fixes what it judges harms enjoyment, but "not everything will be addressed" and some disliked things are accepted as part of the game; genuine bugs should be reported. Confidence: Confirmed (official-stated). Volatility: Low. **Analytical consequence**: this is the first-party answer to "is the game out to get me?" — it confirms randomness/luck and disagreement-inducing variance as designed, while denying differential treatment ("the same for all players"). Combined with GS-002 (no manipulation, no outcome influencing in DLL) and GS-024 (opponent connection cannot degrade your experience), the official position rules out targeted scripting; community "script"/momentum claims remain perceptions that must be tested, not assumed (T-027).
- **GS-032 Emojis in Dream League Live** — six emojis by default; additional earned/purchased emojis must be selected in the "Emoji Selection" menu; only six pre-selected emojis can be used at once, so swapping is required; access via "My Profile" button (team and manager name, top right of the Main Menu) → "Emojis"; disable entirely via Main Menu → gear icon (settings) → "Game" section → "Multiplayer Chat" OFF (article 360020938198). Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: Low. → an anti-tilt control surface: chat can be switched off.
- **GS-033 Custom kits/logos are not transmitted in multiplayer** — imported kits and logos are not transferred across multiplayer matches; the opponent does not see them, and vice versa (article 360021064298). Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: Low. → opponents cannot use custom imagery to distract or offend; also means your custom kit conveys no scouting information.
- **GS-034 User ID retrieval** — Open DLS → Options (gear, top left) → Advanced → System Info (the "i" button) → Copy Info, then paste into a support message. FTG warns: the user is responsible for not loaning, exchanging, sharing, selling or buying game profiles (Terms of Use); do not share personal details — if lost that way, it cannot be recovered (article 37082887837202). Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: Low. → account-security surface; User ID is the support handle, not to be shared publicly.
- **GS-035 Control-button text toggle** — in-match control labels ("Low Kick", "Hard Kick", "Lofted Kick") can be removed via "Game Settings" → "Display" → "Descriptive Button Text" OFF; accessible in-game or from the Main Menu; re-enable by setting it back to ON (article 14369944135058). Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: Low. → a screen-cleanliness option; the labels also reveal the official naming of the three kick types.
- **GS-036 Cannot connect** — DLS requires a connection for most game modes; mobile or Wi-Fi both work but multiplayer needs a stable connection; without an active connection, exhibition matches remain playable (article 360003945557). Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: Low.
- **GS-037 Save-data backup & the services that do NOT back it up** — recommended: link the profile to Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple (iOS 13.0+); this is NOT automatic — it must be done through the game's settings. If the profile is not secured this way, FTG "cannot guarantee that it can be recovered" after an uninstall, device reset or device change. **Save data is NOT secured on Google Play Games or iCloud — these are different cloud services that FTG's games do not use** (article 214387685). For legacy games (DLS19-era), only iCloud (iOS) / Google Play Saved Games (Android) work, and cross-platform transfer is impossible. 8 Ball Hero alone uses Facebook. Confidence: Confirmed. Volatility: Low. → corrects a very common assumption (users believing iCloud/Play Games backs up DLS); this is a high-value account-safety claim.
- **GS-038 Kick taxonomy (from official control labels)** — official button labels name three kick types: Low Kick, Hard Kick, Lofted Kick (GS-035). Confidence: Confirmed (label names official). Volatility: Low. Deeper mechanics (power/aim interaction) still owed to Step 2.
