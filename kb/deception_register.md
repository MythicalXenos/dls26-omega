### DR-001 — sakibpro.com: self-certified accuracy over an undisclosed, admittedly hand-edited data pipeline
kind: authority inflation / unverifiable provenance. opened 2026-09-27T18:22Z (session 1 turn 2). status: OPEN (source not discarded; authority limited).
report: The SakibPro player database asserts its ratings are accurate in blanket terms ("**Yes, absolutely.** Unlike generic sports portals or copycat gaming blogs that paste outdated statistics from previous years..."), describes its process only in pseudo-technical filler ("our player indexing engines process data from active live updates", "Verified base stats, manual overriding adjustments, and tactical updates are systematically synced into the cache structure", "The server engine automatically evaluates and indexes profiles using an aggregate processing metric across both Speed (SPE) and Acceleration (ACC) coefficients"), discloses no extraction method, carries no date and no version stamp anywhere on the page — and admits that "**manual overriding adjustments**" are applied on top of whatever it extracts.
investigation: Investigated by comparison against entries sharing its category and against the prompt's own rules. No prior register entry exists; the closest thing on file is the turn-1 screening note for this domain (incentive observed, capped). Three distinct signals, not one: (a) the party with the most to gain from the claim makes the claim about itself, with no falsifiable criterion; (b) the description of method names no method — every phrase is generic infrastructure language that could describe any site; (c) "manual overriding adjustments" is an admission that the output is partly human-authored while the same breath calls it "verified base stats". Freshness is separately unverifiable: the page claims data is "updated regularly" with no marker, which is exactly the failure mode PROMPT.md's version-capture rule exists to catch, so no claim from it can even be placed against version 13.430.
Does the pattern bear on advice? YES — and that is why this is a register entry rather than only a cap. The site publishes the two numbers a coaching or transfer recommendation would rest on (a "+10 OVR maximum potential" ceiling and "real-world transfer coin costs") and offers tools to compute them. If those numbers were taken as verified, every resulting recommendation would inherit an unverified premise while appearing grounded.
resolution: The source is NOT discarded and its data is NOT assumed false. Its 12-value card-type list independently echoes 8 official FTG terms, which is real corroboration of the *vocabulary* and the reason the page was recorded. What is withdrawn is AUTHORITY: no rating, price, ceiling or stat from sakibpro.com may be stated as a game fact anywhere in this KB; every such value stays capped at Speculative with origin NONE FOUND until it is either echoed by FTG, cross-checked against a second independent database, or confirmed from client assets (Step 2). The pool-size conflict (14,000+ vs FTG's official 4,000+) is logged as an open question with a stated hypothesis (card variants counted as rows) and is not resolved by picking a side. Practical consequence: a SECOND database is now a mandatory frontier item rather than a nice-to-have, because without one there is no cross-check for any third-party number in this project.
saturation_exempt: false

**ESCALATION 2026-09-27T18:40Z (session 1 turn 3, same entry — not a new row):** two further pages on the same domain repeat the pattern with greater specificity, which strengthens rather than weakens the finding.
- `/players/trending.php` (S-0019) presents its own list as "the complete, **officially verified** list of the 17 newly added players" and answers "Is this the official list?" with "**Yes**" — while every official surface reached so far (FTG's site, both store listings, both stores' event pages, S-0002/S-0011/S-0012/S-0015/S-0016/S-0017) publishes **no player list at all**. Official status is borrowed without a source. The page's freshness marker is "Last Updated: September 27, 2026" — the retrieval date — which cannot be distinguished from a dynamically generated date without a Wayback capture (gap G-0022), so freshness remains unverifiable here too.
- `/players/simulator.html` (S-0020) claims access to FTG's unreleased internals five separate ways: "official in-game weight progression", "the **official hidden weighting system**", "accurately mirrors this **official internal logic used by First Touch Games**", "mathematically identical to the live transfer market", "the **precise overflow algorithm** and stat progression logic used internally by the official game". No method, no source, no version stamp. The tool's better tier is gated behind "Pro Simulator 👑", so the incentive is structural: the free tier has to look like a taste of paid certainty. The tool also **generates** card images ("generates a beautiful, official 'Maxed' DLS 26 card"), which means no image from this domain is evidence of in-game appearance.
- Disposition unchanged and sharpened: the domain is NOT discarded (its enumerations are real, specific, internally consistent and independently echo FTG's vocabulary in 8 of 12 card types), but **authority over every number remains withdrawn**. All values stay Speculative with origin NONE FOUND until echoed by FTG, cross-checked by a second independent source, confirmed from client assets (Step 2), or verified by the user in-game — and the user's own coaching screen outranks all of it per the Evidence Hierarchy. Two further consequences adopted this turn: (a) a SECOND database or tool is a mandatory frontier item, because with one source of this kind there is no cross-check for any third-party number in the project; (b) the coaching model is written up in full in `kb/topics/coaching_and_upgrade_system.md` **with its verification plan attached**, because a claim this specific is worth more as a falsifiable hypothesis than as a discarded one.

**REFINEMENT 2026-09-27T18:55Z (session 1 turn 3, same entry):** a per-player card page (S-0021) forces a fairer and sharper characterisation than "authority inflation" alone, and the register records both directions rather than the convenient one.
- **Against the source, and now falsifiable rather than rhetorical:** the card page labels its own headline number "⭐ Base Rating | OVR: 85 **⚠️ ESTIMATED**" — while the site's SEO landing page called the same dataset "the complete, **officially verified** list" (S-0019). The inflated claim sits on the pages written for search engines; the honest caveat sits on the pages written for users. Separately, the site contradicts itself on a checkable fact: de Gea's age is **36** on the trending page and **35** on his card page, and 35 is the one consistent with the DOB the same card page states (November 7, 1990, as of 2026-09-27). One "Similar Players" row renders with an **empty player name** (`/players/classic/player/26841/`), so the dataset has visible holes.
- **For the source, recorded because a register entry that only collects faults is not a finding:** the "⚠️ ESTIMATED" flag, the "✍️ WRONG RATING? SUGGEST FIX" widget and a correction form that **requires a game screenshot** are admissions against interest. A purely deceptive operation would not advertise that its numbers are estimates or invite screenshot-backed corrections.
- **Net disposition (unchanged in effect, refined in reasoning):** this is a **crowd-corrected estimate database presented to search engines as authoritative**. Authority over every number stays withdrawn — the site itself says the numbers are estimates, which settles the question without needing to doubt it. The domain is NOT discarded, for two reasons that are now concrete: its STRUCTURE is informative regardless of whether its values are right (stat-code mapping, card-type URL slugs, multi-version rows per player, an id space reaching down to 153, an "Acquired Via" field naming an acquisition route), and its screenshot-correction mechanism implies **a stream of real in-game screenshots exists behind it** — a potential user-generated evidence channel worth locating, since a screenshot of a coaching screen is closer to primary evidence than any number this site publishes.
- **Consequences adopted:** (a) no third-party numeric claim from this domain enters the KB above Speculative with origin NONE FOUND; (b) its enumerations and mappings are recorded as hypotheses with named confirmation routes; (c) a second independent database or tool remains MANDATORY; (d) where the site's own pages disagree, the disagreement is logged as a conflict and the more internally consistent reading is preferred *as a hypothesis only* — never promoted on consistency alone.

**THIRD REFINEMENT 2026-09-27T20:14Z (session 1 turn 5, same entry): the decisive check ran, and it splits the domain's authority in two.**
SakibPro's Dybala page (S-0027) was compared field-by-field against an in-game capture (kb/evidence/reddit_cult_heroes_dybala_signed_es.jpg): **Speed 81, Acceleration 90, Stamina 80, Strength 62, Control 92, Passing 90, Shooting 90, Tackling 49 — eight of eight — plus OVR 85, height 177 cm, left foot, and the card year 2020. Ten of ten comparable fields match the game itself.** Its own Total (634) sums correctly.
- What this proves: the domain's ROWS behave like extracted client data, not invention. A fabricated database would not match an in-game capture on ten fields including a year banner.
- What it does NOT prove: anything about its other rows (one card is a sample of one), anything about its PROSE (the same page still says "⚠️ ESTIMATED" and the site still claims access to FTG's internals and prints "100% Official" headlines), and nothing at all about the coaching/weighting model, whose numbers no capture has touched.
- The pattern worth naming: **marketing copy and data pipeline can carry opposite truth values inside one artifact.** This site understates its data ("ESTIMATED") while overstating its authority ("officially verified", "100% Official", "official hidden weighting system"). The register's disposition therefore splits: AUTHORITY OVER ROWS is partially restored, exercised case-by-case — a row is promoted only when a second independent source (capture, another database) agrees, per Mechanism 5; AUTHORITY OVER PROSE stays withdrawn in full.
- Consequence for the project's method: per-card second-source checks become the routine promotion path for database rows, and the Isco half of the check (G-0033's second card) is queued so the sample is two rather than one before any generalisation about fidelity is entertained.
