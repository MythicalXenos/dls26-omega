## Turn-7 additions (verified 2026-10-17)
- **M-008 Community tactics compendium (single detailed author, r/DreamLeagueSoccer "Tips/Tricks List", Mar 18 2026 — Community Consensus, one author, unverified by testing):** *Defending* — while holding the tackle button (B), do NOT use the joystick: the player always moves toward the ball; instead tap the screen when level with the opponent to stick a foot in (from behind/at an angle it is likely a foul), or hold a finger on screen when in front to close shots/passes without tackling ("hold position" + shot block). *Anti-wing spam* — do not chase the outermost players as they switch the ball (it leaves your players stranded upfield); switch to defensive mode and run players backwards on every switch to concentrate bodies in your defensive third, making crosses and diagonal box passes easier to defend. *Anti-central-spam* — same idea using central midfielders running backwards; interceptions then convert to counters. *Dribbling* — dribbling at pace then switching direction often produces an unwanted extra touch (dangerous near the touchline); hold the screen for close control just before switching direction to suppress it, release immediately after. *Defending free kicks* — place a player on the goal line; he is switched to quickly after the kick, then use the double-tap trick shot for a header/clearance. *Corners* — keep one player high in your own half as a counter-attack outlet. **Assessment: mechanically plausible and specific — the strongest candidate hypothesis set for Step-4 controlled testing; must not be delivered as advice before testing.**
