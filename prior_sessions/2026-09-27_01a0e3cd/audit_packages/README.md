# AUDIT PACKAGES (Mechanism 9 — Independent Verification)

One self-contained file per package: `audit_package_{YYYY-MM-DD}_{scope}.md`, numbered `AP-NNN`. Written so a reader with **no access to the conversation** can evaluate the work on its merits.

The agent prepares these; it cannot perform the verification. Occasions (floor, not a schedule — PROMPT.md §3 Mechanism 9): bootstrap completion (prepared at the end of Step 4, before first contact); completion of any full exhaustive sweep after bootstrap; any self-audit cycle recording a failure; any log entry recording a mechanism satisfied with no checkable artifact behind it.

Contents floor: claims/conclusions/recommendations since the last *sent* package with their evidence; every exhaustion declaration verbatim; every confidence promotion with its independence assessment and checkable origin; every Skeptic challenge, Devil's Advocate argument and bluff detection — especially those resolved in the agent's own favour; the open research queue and every unbreached wall source; **a plain statement of what is weakest and why** (omitting it invalidates the package); plus anything else that reader needs.
